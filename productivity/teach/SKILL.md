# Teach

Run a deliberately small learning mission. This is a standalone teaching practice, not a course generator, workspace setup routine, or prerequisite for another skill.

## Required Learner Choices

Before creating persistent learning materials, establish these choices with the learner:

1. **Workspace:** the approved path for the mission and learning record.
2. **Mission:** one observable outcome, its scope, and a bound such as a timebox, number of lessons, or completion date.
3. **Trusted sources:** the files, links, books, instructors, or materials that may ground factual claims. Record a source as unavailable rather than silently replacing it.
4. **Learning evidence:** what will demonstrate progress, such as a worked exercise, explanation, project artifact, retrieval quiz, or review of the learner's own work.

Also ask for relevant background, constraints, and accessibility needs only when they affect the mission. Do not select the workspace, mission, sources, or evidence on the learner's behalf.

## Pilot Boundaries

- Limit the pilot to one mission, two to four short lesson-and-practice cycles, and one review point unless the learner explicitly revises the bound.
- Create or update files only in the learner-approved workspace.
- Browser pages, HTML, presentations, and other rendered outputs are optional; use them only if the learner asks.
- Do not claim mastery from attendance, completion, or assistant explanation. Record only demonstrated evidence and the learner's own assessment.
- Do not invoke other skills or assume a phase-based workflow.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and ask the learner to revise the mission or sources when:

- the mission has no observable outcome or cannot fit the chosen bound;
- no trusted source can support a factual claim needed for the lesson;
- the approved workspace is unavailable or not writable; or
- the chosen evidence cannot demonstrate the stated mission.

Separate source-based claims from teaching advice. Label an explanation as guidance when it is not directly supported by a trusted source.

## Create The Mission Record

After the learner approves the four choices, create a concise record in the approved workspace:

```markdown
# Learning Mission: <mission>

## Bound
- Outcome: <observable result>
- Limit: <timebox, lesson count, or date>

## Learner Context
- Starting point: <relevant background>
- Constraints: <constraints>

## Trusted Sources
- <source>: <path or URL> - <topics it supports>

## Evidence Plan
- <evidence artifact or activity> - <what it demonstrates>

## Learning Record
| Cycle | Lesson and source | Practice | Evidence observed | Learner reflection | Next adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

For each cycle, teach only the smallest concept needed for the next practice. Cite the supporting trusted source in the lesson. Ask the learner to produce the agreed evidence, assess that evidence against explicit criteria, and record what was observed without inflating it into a mastery claim.

At the review point, summarize demonstrated evidence, remaining gaps, source limitations, and the learner's chosen next action. The learner decides whether to stop, revise the mission, or start a new bounded pilot.

## Verify Before Reporting

- Confirm the workspace, mission, trusted sources, and evidence plan were selected by the learner.
- Confirm source paths or URLs resolve when accessible, and mark unavailable sources clearly.
- Confirm every factual lesson claim has a cited trusted source or is labeled as guidance.
- Confirm the learning record contains learner-produced evidence, not only assistant explanations.
- Confirm the mission remained within its approved bound or that the learner explicitly revised it.
